Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our services, land and farms, and how to work with us.

Our services

What services does PMIS NIG. LTD offer?

We offer seven services: farm and plantation establishment, agro consultancy and project management, human capacity development, civil and building construction, agro engineering and tools supply, agro land sourcing, and EUDR training and audit. See the Services page for details.

Is PMIS NIG. LTD only focused on agriculture?

Agriculture is a core part of our work, but we also specialize in civil and building construction, human capacity development and agricultural engineering. This multidisciplinary expertise lets us deliver integrated, large-scale solutions for clients.

What is EUDR training and audit?

The EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) requires companies selling certain commodities, rubber among them, into the EU to show that their products are deforestation-free and legally produced. We train your teams and audit your operations so you are ready to meet those requirements.

Do you offer training for farmers and farm teams?

Yes. Our human capacity development and extension services work includes farm-site training, on-the-spot solutions to challenges, and best-practice programmes for farmers and agro-professionals.

Land and farms

Can PMIS help me acquire farmland for agricultural projects?

Yes. We assist individuals and corporate organizations in sourcing farmland for lease or outright purchase. Our land sourcing process prioritizes strategic location, soil suitability and long-term value, to support successful agribusiness operations.

Do you offer farm management services?

Yes. We provide farm management solutions, including planning, daily operations, infrastructure development, irrigation, input supply, pest management and long-term productivity monitoring, to ensure sustainable farm success.

Can you help improve existing farms or plantations?

Yes. We provide farm consultancy and turnaround management for existing farms and plantations. Our team can assess current operations, implement modern techniques, and improve productivity, profitability and compliance with industry standards.

Working with us

Where do you work?

We work across Nigeria, and we serve clients within and outside the country.

How do I engage PMIS NIG. LTD for my project?

Contact us through the website or by phone. We will schedule a consultation to understand your needs, then provide tailored solutions and a clear project roadmap to help you achieve your goals.

Still have a question?

Call, email or send us a message and we will get back to you.
