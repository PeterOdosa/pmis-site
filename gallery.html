EUDR training and audits in the field, with the estates and companies we have worked with.

EUDR compliance training

Helping producers meet EU deforestation rules.

EUDR compliance means making sure agricultural and forest-related products are deforestation-free, legally produced and fully traceable through the supply chain.

It shows a commitment to sustainable production, responsible sourcing and environmental protection, and it keeps access to the European Union market.
