Our History

Our Journey of Growth, One Milestone at a Time

Our story

A story of steady growth and commitment.

Our story is one of steady growth, unwavering commitment, and a vision to transform agriculture, infrastructure and human capacity development across Nigeria.

From humble beginnings to a trusted name in agro-consultancy and project management, our history reflects years of hard work, expertise and partnerships that drive real impact.

Photo · Early plantation work
How we got here

Created in 2010, a limited company since 2017.

  1. August 2010

    P-MAN INTEGRATED SERVICES is created, starting out in:

    • Agricultural management systems
    • Agricultural engineering projects
    • Agro consultancy services
    • Sales and supply of agro chemicals and protective products
    • Civil and building construction works
    • Human capacity development

  2. 2016 to 2018

    560+ farmers and intending farmers trained across four states.

  3. 2017

    The company becomes a limited liability company and takes its present name, P-MAN INTEGRATED SERVICES NIGERIA LIMITED (PMIS NIG. LTD).

Plantations and agribusiness

Construction for multinationals

Human capacity development

Consultancy

Consultancy across several sectors of the economy, mostly for corporate investment clients, with a focus on quality delivery.

Vision and innovation

Best practice that works for farmers and investors.

Our commitment to best practice in agricultural management has aimed at optimizing investors' returns, and has built our reputation for innovation in the industry.

  • New agricultural systems, plus planning and management tools, developed and put into use.
  • An extension services platform that helps farmers improve their methods through farm-site training, on-the-spot solutions to challenges and best practices.
  • Best-practice agricultural management aimed at optimizing investors' returns.
  • Bridging the gap between planters and off-takers for some commodities in Nigeria.
