EUDR Training & Audit

Get your estate, factory or farm network ready for the EU Deforestation Regulation.

See packages Book now
Why it matters

Selling into the EU now means proving where your product comes from.

Keep your market

Rubber, palm oil, cocoa, coffee and other covered products must be deforestation-free and legally produced to be sold in the EU.

Know every plot

Buyers will ask for traceability: where each product was grown, by whom, with the records to back it up.

Prepare your people

Compliance is carried out by farm staff, managers and aggregators. Trained teams make fewer costly mistakes.
