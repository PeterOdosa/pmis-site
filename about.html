About PMIS NIG. LTD

Who we are

P-MAN Integrated Services Nigeria Ltd (PMIS NIG. LTD) is a wholly indigenous company registered under Nigerian law. We are committed to transforming agriculture, construction, and human capital development through innovative, sustainable, and impact-driven services.

Since inception, we've grown from a plantation-focused operation into a multi-sector service provider delivering value to clients across Nigeria. Our work spans farm establishment, agro-consultancy, civil infrastructure, agricultural engineering, and capacity-building solutions, all tailored to deliver lasting results for individuals, institutions, and communities.

Our journey

Our Journey at a Glance

  1. 2017

    PMIS NIG. LTD is founded and registered as a corporate entity in Nigeria.

  2. 2018

    • Developed a ₦1.8 Billion business plan for a 524.5-hectare rubber plantation intercropped with yam for Cemilia Resorts and Farms Ltd, Edo State.
    • Assisted individuals and corporate organizations to acquire farmland for agribusinesses.

  3. 2018 to date

    Continuous supply of fertilizers, chemicals, and tapping tools to farmers across Nigeria.

  4. 2019

    Ongoing consultancy for over 50 farmers in Edo and Ogun States in plantation agriculture and arable farming.

  5. 2020

    Trained over 560 farmers and intending farmers across four states in plantation agriculture, intercropping, and annual/perennial crops.

  6. 2021

    Established a Human Capacity Development Centre for the continuous training and retraining of farmers, including practical demonstrations on project farms.

  7. 2023

    • Consultant to Imoniyame Holdings Ltd for:
      • Due diligence on estate rubber plantations
      • Recruitment and training of managers and supervisors
      • Turnaround management of plantations
      • Establishment of a tapping school
      • Development of a business plan for 5,000 hectares of rubber plantation
    • Became a major supplier of latex cups and spouts to the Rubber Research Institute of Nigeria (RRIN), Edo State.

  8. 2024

    • Managing own 45-hectare rubber plantation, 10.4 hectares for Luckyfad Enterprises, and 3.5 acres of plantain plantation in Edo State.
    • Established a 20-hectare oil palm plantation for a private farmer in Edo State.

  9. 2025

    • Consultant to Okomu Oil Palm Company PLC for:
      • EUDR compliance in rubber sourcing and purchase
      • GPS mapping and documentation
      • Product tracing, EUDR training of aggregators and farmers
      • Field implementation of EUDR requirements for certification

  10. 2026 to date

    EUDR training for Imoniyame Holdings Limited staff, Aggregators and Farmers.
