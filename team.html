Born into the Royal Family of Evboesi, Orhionmwon LGA, Edo State, IGBINOSUN IDOWU PETER is a seasoned agribusiness expert with over 30 years of leadership in agriculture, consultancy, and corporate governance. He holds a degree in Agric extension and Management and a post graduate in Business Administration. He is a certified Auditor in Environmental and Quality Management Systems.

He currently serves as the pioneer National President of the National Rubber Producers, Processors, and Marketers Association of Nigeria (NARPPMAN), and he sits in the Industrial Advisory Panel of International rubber study group in Singapore.

His professional career includes 22 years with Michelin Development Company, where he held senior leadership roles across production, maintenance, agriculture, and project management. He is also a practicing farmer and published author, with notable works including Outgrower Rubber Plantation Establishment and Exploitation (2016) and Investing in Rubber Value Chain in Nigeria (2020).

With extensive local and international experience, he has presented at numerous seminars and workshops and serves on the boards of five reputable Nigerian companies. Through PMIS Nig. Ltd, he continues to drive growth in agriculture, human capacity development, and agro-consultancy across Nigeria.
