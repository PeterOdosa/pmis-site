Who we are

A multi-service Nigerian company building farms, roads and capacity.

PMIS NIG. LTD is committed to transforming Nigeria's agricultural and infrastructure landscapes. With a focus on plantation establishment, agro consultancy, engineering, construction and capacity building, we deliver end-to-end solutions that create value, promote sustainability and build a prosperous future.

What services does PMIS offer?

We offer farm and plantation establishment, agro consultancy and project management, human capacity development, civil and building construction, agro engineering and tools supply, and agro land sourcing.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Yes. We serve clients within and outside Nigeria.

How do I book a consultation?

Use the Contact button, call or email us, and we will arrange a consultation to discuss your goals.

Can you help me find land for an agricultural investment?

Yes. Agro land sourcing is one of our services: we find and facilitate land acquisition for agricultural investments.
